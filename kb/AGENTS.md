# kb

## Purpose

Grounding corpus for external product/API claims — official docs first.

## Ownership

- Owns: sources index, notes, and retrieved materials under `kb/`
- Parent owns: when grounding is mandatory (root Engineering Standards)

## Local Contracts

- `SOURCES.md` lists official URLs and retrieval dates
- `index.json` optional machine-readable index
- Prefer primary vendor docs over blogs
- Never invent product behavior; cite or say unknown

## Work Guidance

- Add a source before relying on a new external claim in deliverables
- Keep snippets short; link out rather than dumping full pages

## Verification

- Every external product claim in active deliverables maps to `SOURCES.md` or is marked unknown
- No credentials in kb files
